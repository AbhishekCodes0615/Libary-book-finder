About Library Book Finder

A smart way to search, explore, and manage books in your library.

Our Mission

Library Book Finder is designed to make it effortless for students, teachers, and library members to discover books quickly. From searching titles, authors, and ISBNs to reserving books, everything is organized in one clean and simple dashboard.

We focus on speed, simplicity, and a friendly interface so that you spend less time searching and more time reading.

Key Highlights

  • Fast search by title, author, category, and ISBN.
  • Simple dashboard for managing your books.
  • Responsive design — works on mobile & desktop.
  • Clean, green theme for a calm reading vibe.

For Students

Quickly find the books you need for your course or interest. Filter by category like Literature, Sci-Fi, Historical, and Computer Science, then add them to your personal list.

For Librarians

Keep track of popular titles, manage reservations, and organize the collection digitally while still keeping the physical library experience alive.

For Everyone

Whether you just love reading or you’re doing research, Library Book Finder helps you explore new books and keep your favourites within reach.

How It Works

  1. Open the Catalogue to browse all available books.
  2. Use the search bar and filters to refine the list.
  3. Open a book’s details to learn more and reserve it.
  4. Track your selected books in the My Books section.

Contact & Support

Have suggestions or found an issue? You can reach the Library Book Finder team through your library staff or system administrator.

This project is created as a smart library management tool to enhance the reading experience and simplify book discovery.
